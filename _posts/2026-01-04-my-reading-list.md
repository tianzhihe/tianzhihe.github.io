This page is my long-term **reading list and knowledge library**.

I use it to keep track of:

- research papers  
- books  
- blog posts  
- news articles  
- technical reports  

For each item, I record:

- **title, authors, and link**
- **published date**
- **accessed / finished date**
- **key takeaway**
- **favorite quote**
- **notes & follow-ups**

---

## How to Read This Page

- Topics on the sidebar Table of Contents help you jump to a domain  
- Each reading item has its own **tabs**:
  - **Key Takeaway** – one or two sentences summarizing why it matters
  - **Favorite Quote** – short excerpt that stuck with me
  - **Notes** – scattered thoughts, bullets, next steps

I will continue expanding this list over time.
