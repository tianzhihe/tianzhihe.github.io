This is my long-term reading log and knowledge library.  
I use it to track blog posts, news articles, research papers, and books that feel important enough to remember.

For each item, I record:

- **Basic metadata:** title, authors, venue, link, and key dates.
- **Key Takeaway:** my one-line (or few-line) summary of why it matters.
- **Favorite Quote:** a passage that stuck with me.
- **Notes:** bullet points, highlights, and follow-up ideas.

Topics are organized as:

- AI  
- 3D / VR / AR  
- Data Center  
- Smart Home  
- Space Habitat
